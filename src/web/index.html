Know the signs

Scams people are getting right now

The details change, but most scams ask for the same things: send money, share a code, or enter a password right away.

Missed delivery

"Pay a small fee to get your package."

Delivery companies don't ask for fees by text.

Unpaid toll

"Pay now to avoid a late fee."

Real toll bills come by mail or in the official app.

Account locked

"Verify your password in 12 hours."

Real companies never ask for your password by email.

Family emergency

"It's me, I'm in trouble. Don't tell Mom."

Call them on their usual number before you send anything.
